### Case Study 1: Building a Personal Financial Plan from Scratch

A 24-year-old software developer with a ₹80,000/month salary (or $2,000/month) wants to:
- Get out of a ₹1.5 lakh credit card debt in 12 months
- Build a 6-month emergency fund
- Start investing for a house down payment (5-year goal)
- Start retirement investing

**Cover in the case study:**
- Full financial snapshot (income, expenses, debts breakdown)
- ASCII cash flow diagram: Income → Fixed Expenses → Debt Repayment → Savings → Investments
- Step-by-step monthly budget plan with exact numbers
- Calculation examples: EMI on the credit card, compound interest on SIP, corpus after 5 years
- Tool recommendations: budgeting apps (YNAB, Walnut), SIP calculators
- Investment strategy: which instruments to choose and why (SIP in index fund, FD for emergency fund)
- Gotchas: lifestyle inflation, forgetting to increase SIP, not having insurance
- Lessons learned

### Case Study 2: Building a Stock Portfolio for Long-Term Wealth

A beginner investor has ₹5 lakhs (or $10,000) saved up and wants to build a long-term equity portfolio over 10+ years.

- Decide on asset allocation (equity, debt, gold)
- Screen and pick 8-10 stocks across sectors using fundamental analysis
- Add index funds and a few ETFs for stability
- Set up a monthly SIP for ongoing investment
- Establish rebalancing rules and review schedule

**Cover in the case study:**
- Portfolio allocation table with reasoning (sector diversification)
- ASCII portfolio breakdown diagram
- Fundamental screening checklist: P/E < 25, ROE > 15%, Debt/Equity < 1, consistent revenue growth
- Stock comparison table with financial metrics
- SIP calculation: monthly ₹10,000 at 12% CAGR over 10/20 years
- Tax strategy: how to minimize capital gains tax using LTCG exemption
- Rebalancing example: when stocks hit 75%, trim and add to debt
- Gotchas: overconcentration in one sector, checking portfolio too often, reacting to news
- Lessons learned
